Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Contact notice: The legal operator name and a dedicated privacy email must be added before public launch. The merchant identity will also appear during checkout and on the payment receipt.

What MIPO processes

  • Room names, player display names, game settings, answers, guesses, scores, chat messages and emoji reactions.
  • Photos that players choose to upload after giving consent.
  • AI-generated images and the limited game context needed to create them.
  • Basic device identifiers, technical logs and first-party analytics events needed for trial limits, purchases, security and product reliability.
  • Payment status and transaction identifiers from PayPlus. MIPO does not receive or store full card details.

How information is used

We use this information to run multiplayer rooms, personalize questions, generate reveal images, restore interrupted games, enforce purchases and free-trial limits, prevent abuse, and measure the basic launch funnel.

Photos and AI

Photos are optional until a room chooses to use image-based features. A player must actively consent before an uploaded photo is saved. Photos and relevant game context may be sent to the configured AI provider to generate content for that private game. AI output can be inaccurate or unexpected.

Retention

Game photos, answers, chat and generated scenes are retained only for active play and short-term recovery, and are scheduled for removal or anonymization after 30 days. Minimal purchase, fraud-prevention and accounting records may be retained longer where required by law. Basic analytics may be retained for up to 14 months.

18+ mode and children

Adult topics are optional and require confirmation from every active player. Family-with-children mode removes adult topics. MIPO is not designed for children to create purchases or provide personal information without an adult responsible for the group.

Your choices

You can avoid uploading a photo, leave a room, or request access/deletion through the verified operator contact that will be published before launch. Some transaction records may need to be retained for legal obligations.

Service providers and international processing

MIPO uses hosting, payment, analytics and AI service providers. Information may be processed in other countries under the provider’s safeguards and applicable law.
