Refund & Purchase Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Support notice: A dedicated purchase-support email and the legal merchant name must be added before public launch. Until then, use the merchant contact shown on the PayPlus receipt.

Digital access

MIPO packages provide digital access that can be consumed immediately. This policy does not reduce any refund or cancellation right that applicable law gives you.

When we will investigate a refund

  • You were charged more than once for the same intended purchase.
  • Payment succeeded but the purchased game did not unlock.
  • A technical failure prevented the group from using the purchased game and support could not restore access.
  • The amount or product charged did not match the confirmed checkout.

How to request help

Contact the merchant shown on your payment receipt and include the transaction date, amount, receipt or transaction identifier, and a short description. Do not send full card details.

Used games and change of mind

After a game has been substantially used, change-of-mind refunds may be limited where applicable law permits. We will assess technical failures and mandatory consumer rights fairly.

Processing

Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method. Bank and card processing times are outside MIPO’s control.
