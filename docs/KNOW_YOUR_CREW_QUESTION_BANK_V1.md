# Know Your Crew — Question Bank V1

Use placeholders {P} = spotlight player; {A}/{B}/{C} = other players. Every question must pass the reaction test before production.

## Warm / Funny — Know Me
1. {P} gets a free flight leaving in three hours. What is most likely: packs in 15 minutes / spends two hours planning / invites someone from this room / stays home?
2. {P} wins ₪100,000 but must spend it in 24 hours. Where does most go: travel / luxury purchase / huge night out / gifts for people?
3. {P} has an unexpected completely free day. What happens first: sleep / spontaneous trip / binge entertainment / organize life?
4. {P} can instantly become world-class at one thing: cooking / music / business / sport. What do they choose?
5. {P} must live for one year in one setting: tropical island / huge city / mountain village / luxury resort. Which one?
6. {P} can delete one daily annoyance forever: traffic / chores / waiting in lines / answering messages. Which disappears?
7. {P} receives a personal assistant for one month. What job gets delegated first: planning / cleaning / messages / shopping?
8. {P} is suddenly famous tomorrow. What are they most likely famous for: business / entertainment / viral accident / reality show?

## Who in the Room — social triggers
9. Who here would {P} call first if something went badly wrong at 3 AM?
10. Who would {P} choose for a one-month trip with no fixed itinerary?
11. Who would {P} trust to plan a surprise birthday without giving instructions?
12. Who would {P} choose as a business partner if they had to split the company 50/50?
13. Who would {P} choose to be stranded with on a tropical island for one month?
14. Who would {P} trust most to keep a secret for ten years?
15. Who would {P} give their unlocked phone to for one hour with the least worry?
16. Who would {P} choose to negotiate on their behalf in an important deal?
17. Who would {P} want beside them on a chaotic road trip where everything goes wrong?
18. Who would {P} choose to make an important decision for them if they were unreachable?

## Personality / habits
19. Which description would {P} choose for themselves: planner / improviser / overthinker / “it’ll work out”?
20. If plans are cancelled at the last minute, what is {P}'s real first reaction: relief / disappointment / immediately make new plans / irritation?
21. When {P} enters a party where they know almost nobody, what happens first: talks to strangers / finds one safe person / observes the room / wants to leave?
22. {P} has to make a big decision tonight. What wins: logic / gut feeling / advice from friends / more research?
23. Which temptation is hardest for {P} to resist: a cheap flight / a great sale / a spontaneous night out / an ambitious new project?
24. If {P} gets lost in a foreign city, what happens: asks someone / maps instantly / enjoys being lost / blames whoever is with them?
25. {P} gets an unexpected compliment from a stranger. What happens: loves it / gets embarrassed / doubts it / tells everyone later?
26. {P} is 30 minutes early. What do they actually do: coffee / walk around / phone / somehow still arrive late?

## Dilemmas
27. {P} chooses: unlimited travel but basic hotels OR luxury hotels but only two trips a year?
28. {P} chooses: know exactly what people think of them OR never worry what anyone thinks again?
29. {P} chooses: double their income but halve their free time OR keep current income and gain an extra free day every week?
30. {P} chooses: live in their dream city with no close friends nearby OR stay near everyone they love in a city they dislike?
31. {P} chooses: always know when someone is lying OR always be able to convince anyone of the truth?
32. {P} chooses: repeat the best year of their life OR skip ahead and see where they are in ten years?
33. {P} chooses: one unforgettable luxury month OR a full year of small spontaneous adventures?
34. {P} chooses: be extremely successful at something they only kind of like OR average at something they love?

## Predict the Crew
35. Who will receive the most votes for “best person to organize a group vacation”?
36. Who will the room vote most likely to make a huge spontaneous life change?
37. Who will the room trust most to handle a crisis calmly?
38. Who will receive the most votes for “would survive longest with no phone”?
39. Who will the room choose as the best person to talk their way out of an awkward situation?
40. Who will receive the most votes for “most likely to turn a hobby into a business”?

42. Who will receive the most votes for “most difficult to surprise”?

## Rank Them
43. {P} ranks the others: most → least likely to agree to a spontaneous flight tomorrow.
44. {P} ranks the others: best → worst at keeping a surprise secret.
45. {P} ranks the others: calmest → most chaotic during a travel disaster.
46. {P} ranks the others: most → least likely to become financially independent first.
47. {P} ranks the others: easiest → hardest to convince to try something new.
48. {P} ranks the others: most → least likely to disappear from the group chat for three days.
49. {P} ranks the others: best → worst person to share a tiny apartment with for a month.
50. {P} ranks the others: most → least likely to make friends with strangers on vacation.

## Stronger late-game questions
51. If {P} could guarantee one person in this room becomes wildly successful, who do they choose?
52. If {P} had to let one person here plan their entire next birthday, who gets the job?
53. Who here does {P} think understands them better than they realize?
54. Who here is {P} most likely to forgive first after a serious argument?
55. If {P} had amazing news they could tell only one person here for 24 hours, who hears it?
56. If {P} had to swap lives with someone here for one week, who would they choose?
57. Who here would {P} most trust to tell them an uncomfortable truth?
58. If everyone moved to different countries tomorrow, who would {P} most likely still speak to every week?

## Callback templates — only from current-game facts
59. Earlier the group labelled {P} “most spontaneous.” Now {P} must plan every detail of a seven-day trip. What will they obsess over first?
60. Earlier {P} chose {A} as their best travel partner. The flight is cancelled and the luggage is gone. Who loses patience first: {P} or {A}?
61. Earlier the group called {P} the most organized. {P} now has 30 minutes to move apartments. What gets forgotten?
62. Earlier the group called {P} the most frugal. {P} must spend ₪50,000 tonight and cannot save or donate it. Where does most go?
63. Earlier {P} trusted {A} with a secret. Now {A} knows something embarrassing but harmless about {P}. What does {P} expect {A} to do: never mention it / tease privately / tell the group eventually / use it as a running joke?
64. Earlier {P} chose {A} for an island. Now there is only one comfortable bed. Who gets it?
65. Earlier the room said {P} handles crises best. A vacation booking disappears at midnight. What does {P} do first: call support / find another hotel / blame someone / laugh and improvise?
66. Earlier {P} was hardest to predict. Give everyone a double-points prediction: which of two deliberately opposite options will {P} choose now?

## Editorial rules
- Never manufacture personal facts.
- Never ask players to disclose trauma, medical information, political/religious views, sexuality, infidelity, illegal acts, body/appearance judgments, or private financial facts in V1.
- Avoid questions whose only payoff is factual recall.
- Prefer a concrete scenario with stakes, forced choice, or social consequence.
- Do not repeatedly target the same relationship pair.
- Balance positive, chaotic and revealing prompts so the game does not become a roast.
- Late-game intimacy should rise only through harmless trust/relationship choices.
